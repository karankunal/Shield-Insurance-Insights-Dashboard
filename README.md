# Shield-Insurance-Insights-Dashboard

## Pilot Project :


- **Project objective:** 

    **1.** Build an end to end _[Power BI Report](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiOWE3MDU0ZGYtMjYzOS00YzAyLTlhNjctZmZhODU0MjgxZWZhIiwidCI6ImM2ZTU0OWIzLTVmNDUtNDAzMi1hYWU5LWQ0MjQ0ZGM1YjJjNCJ9)_

- **Purpose of the project:** Empower businesses to monitor and evaluate their overall business health.

- **Importance of analyzing insurance data:** Identify patterns and track key performance indicators (KPIs).

- **Role of the report:** Determine effective promotion strategies, facilitate effectively handling customers, and identify potential business expansion opportunities and facilitate effeciency.

- **Purpose of insurance analytics:** Evaluation of business performance, support decision-making, and facilitate communication with stakeholders.
